Mobile sidebar directions

Question: on phones, how should the workspace list open so it stops taking over the whole screen? Changing: where the surface lives (inset card, edge drawer, reveal, sheet, two-stage rail), how it moves, and what stays live behind it. Held constant: real workspace rows, template swatches, status markers, copy, Nox Oscura tokens and the four-stop bottom bar. Desktop is untouched.

Try it: swipe right on the Agent (or tap the Workspaces stop) to open. Swipe back, or tap the dimmed content, to close. Tap a workspace to switch. Tap + to see the template picker.
