The bar at rest and mid-swipe

What this compares

The phone has three places side by side: Workspaces ← Agents → Work. Today the bar only shows the visible pane's tabs, so the other two places are invisible until you stumble into them.

Changing between directions

  • Where the cue lives: inside the strip (A, D), on the bar's edge (B), as its own control (C), or on the content (E).
  • How much of the neighbour it shows: a name and count, a position, a label, folded tabs, or just an edge.
  • Whether it moves with your finger while you swipe.

Held constant

  • The real bar: Sidebar · tabs · More, the crossfade and depth swap from nox-layout.css.
  • Design-system tabs, tab strip, content rows, toggles, buttons, status dots; Nox Oscura.
  • Swipe rules from mobile-swipe-controller.ts: flicks commit, slow drags commit past halfway; Work → Workspaces passes through Agents.

Gaps found

  • Toggle has no icon-only options, so C's switch has to spell out "Agents | Work".
  • No small "door" element; A borrows a compact content row with an outline.
  • Icons has no chevron-right; A mirrors Back.
