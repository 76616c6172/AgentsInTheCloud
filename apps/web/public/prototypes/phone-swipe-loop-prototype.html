The bar from Agents to Work

What to judge

  • Does the right-hand list feel like the same place as the left-hand one, reached the other way round?
  • Does the sidebar button sliding across read as "the list is now over here"?
  • Outer edge or Before More: which keeps More where your thumb expects it?

Rules in this prototype

  • Agents: swipe right opens the list on the left, swipe left goes to Work.
  • Work: swipe right goes to Agents, swipe left opens the list on the right.
  • An open list closes by swiping back toward the workspace, tapping it, or tapping the current workspace. It never wraps further.
  • Flicks commit; slow drags commit past halfway (as in mobile-swipe-controller.ts).
