# Codemode for the built-in agent

## Features / enhancements
- The built-in agent has codemode: it writes short scripts that call its tools, running commands in parallel, chaining edits or filtering big outputs. Connect OpenRouter and codemode scripts can also use classifier and image generation models.
- Connect GitLab or Bitbucket alongside GitHub. Private repositories clone in your workspaces, template search covers them, and `glab` and `bkt` are ready to use.
- Changes can show unchanged files too. Open them to edit, or to preview images, video and audio.
- Drag the edge between the history and the diff in Changes to give either more room. It remembers your choice.
- JSON tool results are indented and highlighted.
- `.proto` files are highlighted.

## Bugfixes
- Workspaces open faster, Claude Code and Codex show their history right away, and long conversations stay responsive while they update.
- Scrolling over an inline visual no longer snaps the conversation back to the bottom, and shortcuts keep working after you click into one.
- After you stop the built-in agent, your next message starts a new turn instead of joining the stopped one.
- The Browser view no longer stacks up extra address bars as you navigate.
- A broken network stream in a workspace can no longer crash AgentsInTheCloud.
- Claude Code's Bash timeouts show the right length, like 5 minutes instead of 83 hours.
- When a model provider's sign-in expires, you can sign in again right in its card in Models.
- Hovering a commit in the history shows its full message.
- Method calls get their own syntax colour, and Cappuccino's keywords no longer blend in.
- The scrollbar in fullscreen panes stays at the edge instead of floating mid-screen.
- With Tailscale on, Docker builds in workspaces can look up addresses again, once you've run the installer again.
