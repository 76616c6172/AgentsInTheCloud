## Features / enhancements
- The phone bar stays put and always shows where you are: Workspaces, Agents and Work, with the stop each swipe reaches folded on that side.
- Tap either edge of the phone bar to go where that swipe would take you, including round to the workspace list.
- Busy agents show their workspace's own glyph, rippling in its colour, instead of a spinner.

## Bugfixes
- Tapping a folded phone tab no longer leaves its name scrolled out of view.
