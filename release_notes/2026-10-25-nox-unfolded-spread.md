## Features / enhancements
- Unfold a foldable, or use a tablet, to see your agent and its Work view side by side, split on the fold.
- Swipe left once more to give Work the whole screen; swipe right to bring the agent back, and again for your workspaces beside it.
- Drag the handle between the two halves to give either side more room; it clicks back onto the fold.
- Folding keeps what you were looking at on the cover screen.

## Bugfixes
- A sideways swipe straight after scrolling now switches panes instead of being ignored or taking you back a page.
